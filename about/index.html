About this site

Freedom Ferret collects agendas, agenda packets, and meeting schedules that the public bodies of Shelby County, Tennessee (Shelby County Government, the City of Memphis, the six suburban municipalities, their school boards, and related authorities) post on their official websites. It republishes the extracted text so it can be searched in one place, and links every document back to its official source.

How it works

  • Agenda documents are downloaded from the official posting locations listed in the Boards directory and converted to text. Conversion can introduce errors, especially in scanned pages.
  • Search runs entirely in your browser over a pre-built index. No search terms are sent to any server.
  • Weekly reports summarize upcoming agendas.

Watch-list categories

Agenda lines are scanned for the terms below. A match only means a term appears; it does not describe what an item does or how anyone voted. Read the full item in the official document.

Matching rules: all-lowercase terms are case-insensitive; acronyms in capitals (for example PD, ICE, MOU) only match in capitals as whole words; capitalized terms match as written or in all capitals. Terms must begin and end on word boundaries, with common endings allowed (s, es, ed, ing), so "bond" matches "bonds" but not "vagabond".
