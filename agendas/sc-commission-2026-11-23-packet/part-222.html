88677 911 Wireless Services
88678​
88679It is estimated that about 70 percent of 911 calls are placed from wireless phones, and that percentage
88680is growing. The prompt delivery of wireless 911 calls to public safety organizations benefits the public
88681by promoting safety of life and property. In an effort to improve public safety, the Federal
88682Communications Commission adopted rules aimed at improving the reliability of wireless 911 services
88683and the accuracy of the location information transmitted with a wireless 911 call. Such improvements
88684enable emergency response personnel to ensure that Public Safety Answering Points (PSAPs) receive
88685meaningful, accurate location information from wireless 911 callers in order to dispatch local
88686emergency responders to the correct location and to provide assistance to 911 callers more quickly.
88687​
88688Q: What are the FCC’s Wireless 911 rules and how do they apply?
88689The FCC’s wireless 911 rules apply to all wireless licensees, broadband Personal Communications
88690Service (PCS) licensees and certain Specialized Mobile Radio (SMR) licensees. Mobile Satellite
88691Service (MSS) providers, however, are currently excluded.
88692​
88693The FCC’s basic 911 rules require wireless service providers to transmit all 911 calls to a PSAP,
88694regardless of whether the caller subscribes to the provider’s service or not.
88695​
88696Phase I Enhaced 911 (E911) rules require wireless service providers to provide the PSAP with the
88697telephone number of the originator of a wireless 911 call and the location of the cell site or base station
88698transmitting the call.
88699​
88700Phase II E911 rules require wireless service providers to provide more precise location information to

Lines 88701–88725

88701PSAPs; specifically, the latitude and longitude of the caller. This information must be accurate to within
8870250 to 300 meters depending upon the type of location technology used.
88703​
88704Q: What are the requirements of wireless service providers?
88705The FCC requires wireless carriers to provide more precise location information to PSAPs. Wireless
88706carriers are required to comply with the FCC’s location accuracy rules at either a county-based or
88707PSAP-based geographic level. The standards apply to outdoor measurements only, as indoor use
88708poses unique obstacles.
88709​
88710Wireless service providers are also required to file with the FCC a list of counties, or portions of
88711counties, that they seek to exclude from the location accuracy requirements. The FCC may permit
88712wireless carriers to exclude areas, only where wireless carriers determine that providing location
88713accuracy is limited, or technologically impossible, because of either heavy forestation or the inability to
88714triangulate a caller’s location. Wireless carriers must report any changes to their exclusion lists within
88715thirty days of such changes.
