103The public shall be provided an opportunity to address the Board or Commission during an
104Open Discussion period at the end of each regular and special meeting of the governing body.
105Prior to the start of the meeting, individuals will be required to complete and present to the
106Clerk the Open Discussion Citizen form provided at each meeting. The Open Discussion period
107for regular and special meetings of the Board or Commission shall be limited to twenty (20)
108minutes. Individuals shall be allowed to speak for up to three (3) minutes each. Open
109Discussion periods will not be held for any meeting where there are no actionable items on the
110agenda or meetings where the governing body is conducting a disciplinary hearing for a
