102 RESOLUTION TO ESTABLISH IMPOUNDMENT AND STORAGE FEES FOR
103 VEHICLES PURSUANT TO SECTION 15-704 OF THE MILLINGTON MUNICIPAL
104 CODE
105​
106 WHEREAS, the City of Millington has adopted regulations governing the impoundment
107and storage of vehicles under Title 15, Chapter 7 of the Millington Municipal Code; and
108​
109 WHEREAS, Ordinance 2026-18 amended Section 15-704 to provide that impoundment
110and storage fees shall be established by resolution of the Board of Mayor and Aldermen; and
111​
112 WHEREAS, the Board of Mayor and Aldermen desires to establish fees for the
113impoundment and storage of vehicles.
114​
115 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF MAYOR AND
116ALDERMEN OF THE CITY OF MILLINGTON, TENNESSEE, that the following fees are
117hereby established pursuant to Section 15-704 of the Millington Municipal Code:
118​
119 Impoundment/Towing Fee: $375.00 per vehicle, assessed as a one-time fee.
120​
121 Storage Fee: $40.00 per vehicle for each twenty-four-hour period the vehicle remains in
122 storage.
123​
124 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that impoundment and storage fees may be waived in
125accordance with the provisions of Section 15-704 of the Millington Municipal Code.
