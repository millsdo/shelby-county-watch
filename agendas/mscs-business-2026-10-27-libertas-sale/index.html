14Recommendation: It is recommended that the declaration of surplus and sale of the former Georgian Hills Elementary
15School, 3930 Leweir, 38127, to Libertas School of Memphis in the amount of $1,000,000.00 be approved by the Shelby County
16Board of Education.
17Purpose/Description and Background: This item declares surplus and sells a closed school property to a state-approved
18charter school under T.C.A. 49-13-136. Georgian Hills Elementary School closed in 2026. The building is 48,307 square feet with
1926 classrooms, constructed in 1951, in Board District 3, and sits on a shared campus with Georgian Hills Middle School. Estimated
20ten-year maintenance cost is $3 million. The property appraised at $1,220,000; the offer is $1,000,000. Libertas School of
21Memphis proposes to open a state-approved micro-Montessori high school serving up to 250 students.
22District Goal the Request Serves: Priority 4: Strengthen operational excellence, transparency, and financial accountability
23across the district through modern systems, internal controls, and effective governance.

27Measurable Outcomes Expected: Outcome 1 — Sale closed and $1,000,000.00 in proceeds received by February 28,
282027. Outcome 2 — Annual security monitoring and grounds and building maintenance costs of $11,685.00 removed from the
29Facility Services budget beginning FY28. Implementation 1 — Former Georgian Hills ES removed from district real estate
30inventory within 10 days of closing.
31Expected Benefits & Why This Is the Best Purchase: The sale avoids an estimated $3 million in ten-year maintenance
32on a 1951 building the district no longer uses, returns $1,000,000 to the district, and removes a vacant building from a campus
33shared with an operating middle school. It also keeps a state-approved charter high school in the Frayser community.
34Other Options Considered: Retaining and maintaining the building was rejected: it carries an estimated $3 million ten-
35year cost and ongoing security monitoring with no district use. Demolition was considered — estimated cost $850,000 with no
36offsetting revenue. One other interested buyer visited the property.
37Adverse Impact If Not Approved: A vacant building would remain in the community on a shared campus with Georgian
38Hills Middle School, the district would not realize $1,000,000 in revenue or shed the maintenance liability, and Libertas School
39would purchase another Frayser facility for its approved charter high school.
