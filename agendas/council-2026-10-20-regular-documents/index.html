170AS ADOPTED BY THE CITY OF MEMPHIS ON AUGUST 10, 2010, AND AS AMENDED, TO ESTABLISH A
171LAND-USE AND SITING FRAMEWORK FOR DATA CENTERS AND OTHER RESOURCE-INTENSIVE USES
172WITHIN THE CITY OF MEMPHIS.

174WHEREAS, the Memphis and Shelby County Unified Development Code (“UDC”) establishes the zoning
175and subdivision regulations applicable within the City of Memphis; and

183WHEREAS, technological and economic development has resulted in new land uses, including large-

185cooling, backup-generation, and land-use characteristics may differ substantially from conventional
186office, commercial, and industrial development; and
