55​
56BACKGROUND: In 1952, Shelby County allowed for two principal structures to be
57constructed on the four (4) acre subject property. The subject property was
58annexed into the City of Bartlett in 2005. Article VI, Section 14 of the Bartlett
59Zoning Ordinance limits single family residential lots to no more than one (1)
60primary structure. The subject property has been considered a pre-existing
61nonconforming lot of record since being annexed into the City of Bartlett due to
62having two (2) principal structures on a single family residential lot.
63​
64DISCUSSION: The specific request by the applicant is for Final Plan approval for
65the Delgado Subdivision to subdivide the four (4) acre subject property into two
66lots so each pre-existing primary structure may remain on each lot and eliminate
67the pre-existing nonconforming lot of record status. Lot 1 is 1.007 acres and is
68the current site of the single family residential structure addressed as 4950
69Germantown Road. Lot 2 is 2.981 acres and is the current site of the single family
70residential structure addressed as 4956 Germantown Road. Access to the
71subdivision will be from the existing driveways on Germantown Road.
