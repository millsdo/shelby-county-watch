1464(2) The neighborhood meeting shall generally comply with the following procedures:
1465 (a.) Time and place. The neighborhood meeting shall be held either at a place that is generally accessible to neighbors
1466 that reside in close proximity to the land subject to the application or virtually online. It shall be scheduled after 5:00
1467 p.m. and on a Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday and not on a day when Town Hall is closed for a holiday.
1468 (b.) Notification. The applicant shall provide notification of the neighborhood meeting a minimum of 14 ten business days
1469 in advance of the meeting by mail, to all property owners and occupants within 500 feet of the land subject to the
1470 application, the Board of Mayor and Aldermen, and the Town Planner. The notification shall state the time and place
1471 of the meeting. A copy of the neighborhood meeting notification and list of property owners and occupants
1472 contacted shall be provided with the development application, with the development application considered
1473 insufficnet without this information. The Town Planner shall additionally provide notice of the neighborhood
1474 meeting on the Town’s website and to the Board of Mayor and Aldermen at least seven days in advance of the
1475 neighborhood meeting.
