629 (A) Authority. In accordance with § 151.308, the Board of Zoning Appeals has the authority to hear
630and act upon applications for variances to alleviate hardships by virtue of the inability of the landowner
631to comply strictly with the provisions of this chapter by reasons of unique shape, topography or physical
632features of the property.
633 (B) Purpose. The purpose of the variance is to modify the strict application of the specific
634requirements of this chapter in the case of exceptional irregular, narrow, shallow or steep lots or other
635exceptional physical conditions, whereby strict application would result in practical difficulty or undue
636hardship which would deprive an owner of the reasonable use of his or her land. A variance may only
637be granted if it will not cause substantial detriment to the public good and without substantially impairing
638the intent of the Zoning Code.

738§ 151.311 SITE PLAN REVIEW.
739 (A) Purpose and scope. The purpose of the site plan review procedure is to ensure compliance with
740the town’s development requirements and its design guidelines and to encourage quality development
741reflective of the guiding principles of the land use plan. Building permits shall not be issued, nor shall
742any site preparation commence for uses requiring site plan review until the site plan is approved
743pursuant to this chapter and approved by the Development Director. There are established submittal
744requirements and procedures for the review and approval of preliminary and final site plans by the town
745as defined in § 151.003. Preliminary and final site plans are not to be considered as being synonymous
746with subdivision plats as defined in the subdivision regulations of the town. Site plans dependent upon
747minor or major, will be reviewed by: the Development Director, Planning Commission (PC), Design
748Review Commission (DRC), Historic District Commission (HDC) (for parcels located within the Historic
749District) and the Board of Mayor and Aldermen (BMA). The procedures and requirements set forth
750within this chapter shall provide for the timely and effective review of development proposals by town

753 (B) Initiation. An application for site plan approval may be initiated by the owner or entities having a
754contractual interest in the property for which site plan approval is requested or by the authorized agent
755of such owner or other person.
756​
757 (C) Applicability. The following shall require site plan review and approval:
758 (1) All new buildings and building additions.
759 (2) All manufactured buildings to be moved to a parcel except prefabricated accessory structures
760placed on single-family residential parcels or lots in residential zones.
761 (3) Site alterations, including the construction of driveways, loading areas and parking areas, with
762the exception of single family residences.
763​
764 (D) Exemptions. The following shall be exempted from site plan review:
765 (1) Individual single-family detached dwellings and their accessory structures.
766 (2) The internal construction or change in floor area of a development that does not increase gross
767floor area, increase the intensity of use, or affect parking requirements on a site that meets all
768development and site design standards of the ordinance codified herein.
769 (3) Temporary uses.
770 (4) Subdivisions.
771 (5) Townhouses, where each individual housing unit is located on a single, platted lot in an
772approved subdivision.
773 (6) Duplexes, where only one duplex structure is located on a single, platted lot in an approved
774subdivision.
