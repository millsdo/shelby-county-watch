1796VECA respectfully requests that Memphis City Council review and reverse the LUCB's

1803GROUNDS FOR APPEAL
1804I. Compliance with Required Neighborhood Notification Procedures
1805Section 9.3.2(B) of the UDC establishes notification requirements for neighborhood
1806meetings associated with major subdivision applications.
1807​
1808Under Section 9.3.2(B)(2), the applicant is required to contact officers of qualifying
1809registered neighborhood associations before determining the date of the required
1810neighborhood meeting.
1811​
1812VECA, the recognized neighborhood association representing the area in which the
1813subject property is located, was not contacted before the meeting date was established
1814and did not receive the required neighborhood meeting notification through its officers.
1815​
1816This raises a material question regarding compliance with the neighborhood notification
1817procedures applicable to this subdivision.
1818​
1819Furthermore, Section 9.3.2(B)(5) provides that an application may be considered
1820incomplete and removed from the LUCB agenda when the Zoning Administrator
1821determines that the required neighborhood notification package is incomplete.
1822​
1823VECA requests that City Council examine the applicant's notification documentation,
1824including the mailing list, dates of mailing, notice recipients, and evidence of contact
1825with the neighborhood association, to determine whether the notification requirements

1855III. Application of UDC Section 3.9.2(D) — Contextual Lot Width
1856Section 3.9.2(D) establishes contextual standards for determining permissible
1857reductions in minimum residential lot widths based on surrounding properties.
1858​
1859VECA examined all 19 parcels along Faxon Avenue between Hawthorne Street and
1860McLean Boulevard, including eight parcels on the north block face and 11 parcels on
1861the south block face.
1862​
1863The eight parcels on the north block face, including the subject property, have an
1864average width of 54.38 feet. The 11 parcels on the south block face have an average
1865width of 41.80 feet. The combined average of all 19 parcels is approximately 47.09 feet.
1866​
1867VECA also examined the four closest neighboring parcels, which were previously
1868calculated to have an average width of 62.5 feet. This additional calculation further
1869illustrates the established development pattern surrounding the subject property.
