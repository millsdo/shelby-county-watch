1432 Existing Conditions
1433 The subject property is 60 feet wide and 160 feet deep (approximately [0.22] acres). It is zoned
1434 R-6 and is currently vacant lot. The property is served by a public alley along its rear line and is
1435 within the Vollintine-Evergreen Historic District.
1436​
1437 Proposed Subdivision
1438 The applicant proposes to divide the property into two single-family residential lots, each 30 feet
1439 wide and 160 feet deep. Vehicular access for both lots will be from the rear alley. No new curb
1440 cuts or driveways on Faxon Avenue are proposed, which keeps the continuous sidewalk, street
1441 trees, and front-yard landscape along Faxon intact, consistent with the historic development
1442 pattern of the block.
1443​
1444 Consistency with Memphis 3.0
1445 The proposal supports the Memphis 3.0 Comprehensive Plan by directing new housing to
1446 existing neighborhoods served by existing infrastructure rather than to the urban fringe. Two
1447 modest single-family homes are consistent with that designation and with the walkable, compact
1448 lot pattern found throughout Midtown. The project uses the existing street, alley, and utility
1449 network, requires no public infrastructure expansion, and adds housing in a transit-served,
1450 walkable part of the city.
