3333Oak & Ember Wellness LLC respectfully submits this revised Letter of Intent in support of its
3334application for a Special Use Permit to operate a structured sober living residence at 3397 Southern
3335Avenue. This letter replaces the Letter of Intent filed on September 23, 2026, and reduces the requested
3336occupancy to no more than eight (8) residents.
3337The property is an existing two-story, eight-bedroom residence of approximately 3,768 square feet on a
33380.27-acre lot. The property is owned by Jesse Rowsley, as Trustee of the One Way Our Way Revocable
3339Living Trust dated January 10, 2020 (Warranty Deed recorded December 10, 2025, Shelby County
3340Register Instrument No. 25105786). Mr. Rowsley is also a member of Oak & Ember Wellness LLC and
3341consents to this application. The residence will open following approval and completion of renovations
3342now underway under permitted work.
3343Oak & Ember will provide a safe, structured, peer-supported home for adult men in recovery from
3344substance use disorder. Our mission is to help residents build stable, sober, and productive lives as they
3345return to work, family, and community.
3346Operational Plan
3347Occupancy
3348The residence will house no more than eight (8) residents, supported by a House Manager who lives at
3349the property. Residents live together as a single household, sharing the kitchen, common areas, and
3350household responsibilities. No additions or exterior changes to the building are proposed.
