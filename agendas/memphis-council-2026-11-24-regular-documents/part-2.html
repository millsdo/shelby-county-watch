513Description:
514The subject property is known as Lot 10A within the Nonconnah Corpate Center Subdivision and zoned
515Employment (EMP). Per the Assessor’s website, the subject property has a hotel constructed circa 1979 with a
516building square footage of 69,910. The subject property is surrounded by commercial and industrial zoning
517and/or land uses.
518​
519Site Plan Review
520The proposed site plan shows the existing hotel and 195 existing parking spaces. Of these, 169 are standard
521parking spaces, 2 are designated for electric vehicles (EV), and 8 are accessible ADA parking spaces, for a total
522of 179 on-site parking spaces. The site also contains a nonconforming curb cut that is no longer in use. The
523applicant is required to close the curb cut and replace it with a sidewalk.

525Analysis

Lines 526–550

526The applicant is requesting a special use permit to allow a hotel in the Employment (EMP) zoning district. The
527hotel currently exists and previously operated as a legal nonconforming use until operations ceased for more
528than 365 consecutive days. Pursuant to the Unified Development Code (UDC), a nonconforming use that has
529been discontinued for more than 365 days loses its legal nonconforming status.
530​
531Because the nonconforming status has expired, the applicant is required to obtain a special use permit to
532reestablish hotel operations. Hotels are not permitted by right within the Employment (EMP) zoning district;
533therefore, approval of a special use permit is required before the use may resume.
534​
535The site has historically functioned as a hotel, and the surrounding area has previously accommodated the
536associated traffic, parking, and operational characteristics of the use. No significant changes to the site's layout
537or intensity of development are proposed. Therefore, staff finds that allowing the hotel to resume operations
538through the special use permit is compatible with the surrounding area and is not expected to create adverse
539impacts beyond those that previously existed.
540​
541The proposal is also consistent with the Memphis 3.0 General Plan per the land use decision criteria (see pages
54222–24 of this report for additional details).
543​
544The granting of this special use permit will not cause substantial detriment to the public good, nor will it
545substantially impair the intent and purpose of an adopted plan or the Unified Development Code (UDC), nor will
546it be injurious to the neighborhood or the general welfare, and it will be in harmony with the purpose and intent
547of the UDC.
