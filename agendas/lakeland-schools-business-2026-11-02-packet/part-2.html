514Local education agencies (LEAs) are required to comply with all federal and state education laws
515and State Board of Education (SBE) rules. This annual compliance report is one mechanism the
516department uses to ensure education laws and rules are faithfully executed. The commissioner of
517education is charged with taking corrective action when an LEA is noncompliant with those
518laws and rules or is not following a department-approved compliance plan.
519​
520Each LEA must submit this report and, if applicable, the corresponding corrective action plan, to
521the department by November 30, 2026. During report completion, an LEA should carefully check
522the status of its compliance with all federal and state education laws and SBE rules. The
523department monitors and verifies LEA compliance via multiple data sources (e.g., internal program
524managers) and will consider those sources in making a final determination of an LEA’s compliance.
525Please be advised that annual compliance report data may inform an LEA’s approval classification.
