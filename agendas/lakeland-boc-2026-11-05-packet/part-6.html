2081DISCUSSION
2082Ensuring the City's Code of Ordinances reflects the current laws and policies of the City
2083requires two types of maintenance processes: Annual Code Update and Recodification. The
2084Annual Code Update is a routine process where a municipality updates its existing municipal
2085code once a year. It incorporates all new ordinances and charter amendments passed over the
2086previous year. A Recodification is a comprehensive overhaul of the municipal code. It involves
2087drafting new codes from model versions, converting or re-organizing earlier codes, and
2088compiling a brand-new structured master code rather than just slotting in recent changes.
2089Municipal Technical Advisory Service (MTAS) recommends recodification every ten years.
2090​
2091The City is preparing to submit the Annual Code Update to MTAS in January 2027. The
2092recodification of the Code of Ordinances has not been completed since 2002. This resolution
2093authorizes the City of Lakeland to engage MTAS to recodify the City’s Code of Ordinances.
2094Due to the current MTAS workload, the recodification is expected to begin November 2027.

2152MTAS provides two classes of codiﬁcation services: an annual update service and a complete code service.
2153​
2154The annual update service includes updating each city’s code once a year by incorporating all of the
2155ordinances adopted during the past year and any amendments made to the city’s charter. All cities
2156that take advantage of this service will pay an annual fee of $200, which includes the web hosting of
2157the charter and full code as well as 10 copies of the updated pages. Additional copies will be invoiced
2158separately based on our actual costs for duplication, dividers and shipping. When a city that is web hosted
2159by MTAS submits ordinances to revise the code, the city will be charged twenty-one dollars ($21) per page
2160based on the number of pages that are modiﬁed by the ordinances. For cities who do not have an MTAS
2161web-hosted code, the city will be charged twenty-ﬁve dollars ($25) per page based on the number of pages
2162that are modiﬁed by the ordinances. The per-page fee allows for a cost that is based on the amount of work
2163done on each update. MTAS will invoice cities for updates when the work is completed and sent to the city.
2164​
2165The second service is for the creation of a complete code, which includes new codes from scratch, all
2166conversions and re-codiﬁcation of old MTAS codes and any conversion of a code prepared by a private
2167code company. Charges for complete codes services are based on population because of the relative work
2168load and the ability of the city to pay. MTAS will send an invoice to cities for 50 percent of the cost
2169of a complete code when we are ready to begin work on the code, usually three to four months before
2170completion. MTAS will invoice cities for the remaining 50 percent upon delivery.
