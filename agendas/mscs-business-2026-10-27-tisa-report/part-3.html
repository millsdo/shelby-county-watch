1078Overview
1079The Tennessee Investment in Student Achievement (TISA) public school funding formula marks a significant change in how Tennessee invests in public
1080education. The TISA funding formula updates the way Tennessee funds public education for the first time in over 30 years to empower each student to read
1081proficiently by third grade, prepare each high school graduate for postsecondary success, and provide resources needed for all students to ensure they
1082succeed.
1083 As part of TISA, T.C.A. § 49-3-112 requires each school district, starting in the 2023-24 school year, to submit an annual accountability report to the
1084 Tennessee Department of Education (department). This report must include:
1085 • Goals for student achievement which are now addressed through the LEA plan. One of the goals must include the district's plan to pursue the goal of
1086 seventy percent (70%) or more of the district's third grade students to score "met expectations" or "exceeded expectations" on the English Language Arts
1087 (ELA) portion of the TCAP tests. This goal must also detail the district's goal to increase 3rd grade ELA proficiency rates by 15% of the gap over three years
1088 (starting with the 2022-23 TCAP results) to achieve the district's stated goal of at least 70% of 3rd grade students proficient in ELA.
1089 • Explanation of how the district's stated goals can be met within the district's budget which are now addressed through the LEA plan.
1090 • For reports submitted starting in the 2024-25 school year, a description of how the district's budget and expenditures from the prior school year enabled the
1091 district to make progress toward the stated student achievement goals. Prior year reflection and expenditures are to be completed at the beginning of each
1092 school year as a separate section, now located within the LEA planning process.
1093Each district's TISA accountability report, including goals, strategies, and reflections, are required to be presented to the public for review and comment before
1094the report is submitted to the department.
1095Additionally, the TISA Accountability must be presented for local school board approval. The report must be submitted annually to the department by November
10961st.
1097Furthermore, each district's TISA accountability report is required to be reviewed annually by the TISA Progress Review Board pursuant to T.C.A. § 49-3-114 to
1098determine whether the school district is taking the proper steps to achieve their stated goal.
1099For questions, please review the TISA Accountability Report Guidance document or contact tnedu.funding@tn.gov.
1100Completed reports should be submitted in ePlan by November 1, 2026.
