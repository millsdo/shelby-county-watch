Revised agenda, first collected Sun Nov 22, 2026. SUP 26-17 hotel at 2736 S. Nonconnah; first readings of 2027 Council meeting dates and Ord. 5991 adding 5C Data Center to Community Benefits Ord. 5953; MLGW fiscal consent (Tennergy 31-year gas prepay contract, Lehman-Roberts paving ratification/increase $23,025,201.70, HPE sole-source ratification $3,081,868.66, Mansfield Oil PO ratification $25,000,000, bond professionals); third and final reading of Ord. 5989 (data center community benefit agency); First Amendment to the Legacy at Countrywood economic impact plan (TIF); $12,500 grants-to-sponsorship transfer; 2027 Council chair and vice chair. Public hearing: (None). The MLGW gas/water rate increase resolutions are on the MLGW committee agenda but not on this revised regular agenda. Outcome (news, Nov 24: Memphis Report, FOX13, Daily Memphian, Commercial Appeal): MLGW rate items were added at the meeting; the Council set the ratemaking public hearing for Dec 8 and held the gas and water rate resolutions until then. Water Utility Relief Program ($4M over three years; $1,333,000 in FY2027) passed with Cooper-Sutton abstaining. Easter-Thomas elected 2027 chair and Spinosa vice chair. Results on Ord. 5989 (third reading), Ord. 5991 (first reading) and the Countrywood TIF amendment were not clearly reported; no minutes posted as of Wed Nov 25.
