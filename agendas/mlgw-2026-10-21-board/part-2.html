763PRESIDENT’S LETTER
764As Memphis and Shelby County continue to grow, Memphis Light, Gas
765and Water remains focused on one essential responsibility: delivering
766reliable, resilient and affordable utility services while preparing our
767systems for the future. The proposed 2027 budget advances that
768commitment through disciplined investment in infrastructure,
769technology, employees and customer service.
770​
771This budget builds on the progress MLGW has made through preventive
772maintenance, infrastructure renewal and replacement, grid modernization
773and system expansion. Continued investment in our electric, gas and water systems is necessary to
774strengthen reliability, reduce service disruptions and ensure that our infrastructure can support both
775current needs and future economic development.

Lines 776–800

776​
777Our employees remain central to every improvement we make. The 2027 budget supports the
778workforce, tools and training required to operate increasingly complex utility systems and serve
779customers with professionalism and care. It also strengthens our ability to engage with customers,
780businesses, elected officials and community partners through transparent communication and
781meaningful collaboration.
782​
783The proposed 2027 operating and capital budget totals $2.6 billion. Of that amount, $1.4 billion is
784allocated to purchased power and gas costs. Operations and maintenance expenses total $622
785million, and our capital program is budgeted at $371 million. These investments reflect our
786responsibility to manage public resources carefully while addressing the infrastructure and
787operational needs that affect customers every day.
788​
789MLGW has made meaningful progress, but our work continues. Through responsible stewardship,
790strategic planning and sustained investment, we will strengthen the systems our community depends
791on and position MLGW to serve future generations.
