7180MAXIMUM DAILY DEMAND
7181The Maximum Daily Demand shall be defined as the maximum volume consumed during a twenty-four (24) hour
7182period during the current month but shall not be less than 80% of the Maximum Daily Demand for any of the preceding
7183eleven (11) months, provided that gas consumed during the months of May through September for the preceding
7184eleven (11) months shall be excluded when determining the Maximum Daily Demand for this purpose. The Maximum
7185Daily Demand shall be determined at the option of the Division according to one of the following methods:
7186 1. By estimate – one-twentieth (1/20) of the current monthly consumption
7187 2. By actual test
7188 3. By demand meter
