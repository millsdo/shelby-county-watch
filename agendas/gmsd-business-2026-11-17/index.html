77C. Financial Report

85D. Superintendent's Report
86 Superintendent Jason Manuel presented the district's updated Five-Year Strategic Master
87 Facility Plan, including enrollment trends, capital improvement priorities, and long-range
88 facility planning.
89 GMSD reported enrollment of 5,790 students based on the first 20-day count, reflecting
90 overall enrollment stability. Since the district's establishment, more than $113 million has
91 been invested in school facilities through various funding partnerships.
92 The proposed five-year plan outlines facility improvements, including HVAC upgrades,
93 painting, flooring, parking lot improvements, and athletic facility projects. Superintendent
94 Manuel emphasized that the plan identifies recommended priorities and does not represent
95 guaranteed funding.
96 The complete plan is available on the district website under the Operations Department.
