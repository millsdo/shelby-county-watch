51574.10.4 Planned Residential Developments
5158In addition to the standards and criteria set forth in Section 4.10.3, planned residential developments shall
5159comply with the standards and criteria set forth below:
5160 A. Formal Open Space
5161 A minimum of 0.6% of the total land area of a planned residential development of 15 acres or more
5162 shall be subject to the formal open space requirements of Section 6.2.3. No open area may be
5163 delineated or accepted as formal open space under the provisions of this Chapter unless it meets the
5164 standards of Chapter 6.2, Open Space.
5165 B. Accessibility of Site
5166 All proposed streets, alleys and driveways shall be adequate to serve the residents, occupants, visitors
5167 or other anticipated traffic of the planned residential development. The location of the entrance
5168 points of the streets, alleys and driveways upon existing public roadways shall be subject to the
5169 approval of the City or County Division of Public Works.
5170 C. Off-Street Parking
5171 Off-street parking shall be conveniently accessible to all dwelling units and other uses. Where
5172 appropriate, common driveways, parking areas, walks and steps may be provided, maintained and
5173 lighted for night use. Screening of parking and service areas shall be required through use of trees,
5174 shrubs and/or hedges and screening walls.
5175 D. Pedestrian Circulation
