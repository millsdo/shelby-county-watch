691SUBJECT: Review of Bids and Award of Contract for ADA Ramp Upgrades
692​
693INTRODUCTION:
694The purpose of this agenda item is to award a contract for the Town of Collierville’s ADA Ramp
695Upgrades Project to Enscor, LLC.
696​
697BACKGROUND:
698The ADA Ramp Upgrades Program began in FY25 and is funded through the Town’s State Street Aid
699Fund. The program replaces non-compliant curb ramps one (1) year before the Town’s annual street
700paving program. Completing these improvements ahead of paving helps avoid damaging newly paved

Lines 701–725

701streets and ensures the ramps meet ADA requirements.
702​
703The program improves pedestrian safety and accessibility while helping the Town maintain compliance
704with federal standards. During the FY26 project, the Town upgraded 71 non-compliant ADA ramps.
705​
706DISCUSSION:
707The current project includes ADA curb ramp upgrades in the Tara Oaks, Cottonwood, Eagles Nest, and
708Nolley Plantation subdivisions. Work includes removing old ramps, installing new ADA-compliant
709ramps with detectable warning surfaces, grading, erosion control, traffic control, and restoring the work
710areas. Completing these improvements before future paving will help prevent damage to newly paved
711streets while improving pedestrian safety and accessibility.
712​
713Bids for the project were opened on September 23, 2026, and seven (7) proposals were received. After
714reviewing the bids, Enscor, LLC. was determined to be the lowest responsive and responsible bidder.
715Their proposal included a base bid of $133,850.00, Add Alternate #1 for $24,200.00, and Add Alternate
716#2 for $33,100.00. Enscor, LLC. has the experience and qualifications needed to complete the project
717within the 60-day construction period.
718​
719The project has a budget of $324,000 through the State Street Aid Fund. The recommended contract
720amount of $191,150.00, including both alternates, is well below the approved budget. Staff recommends
721awarding the contract to Enscor, LLC. for $191,150.00. This project will help the Town address ADA
722requirements, improve pedestrian accessibility, and prepare streets for future paving. The bid evaluation
723summary and contract are attached for review.

771On November 24, 2025, the Board of Mayor and Aldermen approved a contract with Routeware, Inc. to
772provide automated routing software for the Sanitation Division. The original agreement included nine
773tablets, front-office software, and associated services at a first-year cost of $33,480.00.
774​
775DISCUSSION:

Lines 776–800

776Since implementation, Routeware has proven beneficial in improving route efficiency, documenting
777recycling contamination, and assisting staff with daily collection operations. The software has allowed
778staff to monitor route completion times and rebalance several collection routes to improve efficiency
779and accommodate continued residential growth. These improvements have eliminated the need for an
780additional recycling help truck on Wednesdays. The software has also improved documentation of
781recycling contamination, resulting in the removal of approximately 30 recycling carts over the past year.
782​
783The proposed contract amendment will expand the system from nine to 18 tablets. The additional tablets
784will be utilized in five brush trucks, three rear-loading trucks, and one vehicle for recycling cart audits
785or collection assistance. During leaf collection season, staff also plans to temporarily relocate tablets
786from brush trucks to leaf collection vehicles to better track collection progress and document service-
787related issues.
788​
789Funds were included in the FY27 Sanitation Division budget in anticipation of this amendment. Staff
790anticipates similar operational improvements as the software is expanded throughout the remainder of
791the Sanitation fleet.
