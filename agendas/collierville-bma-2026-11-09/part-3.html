857DISCUSSION:
8581. According to the Zoning Ordinance, industrial districts are designed to promote and protect
859the health, safety, morals, convenience, order, prosperity and other aspects of the general welfare.
860 The Town has two (2) types of industrial zoning district (GI: General Industrial & RI: Restrictive
861 Industrial). The RI zoning district is designed to provide space for a wide range of industrial and
862 related uses which conform to a high level of performance standards and have the least
863 objectionable characteristics. RI zoning district is used as a buffer between other districts and
864 other industrial activities which have more objectionable influences.
865 All operations within the RI zoning district must be carried on within completely enclosed
866 buildings. This prevents any adverse characteristics from affecting neighboring properties.
867 Exceptions could include customary outdoor display of heavy equipment for sale or rent.
868 The GI zoning district is designed to provide space for a wide range of industrial and related uses
869​
870 which by reasons of volume of raw materials or freight, scale of operation, type of structures
871 required or other similar characteristics require locations relatively well segregated from
872 nonindustrial uses. Outdoor activity is common in GI districts.
873​
8742. The applicant has requested to amend section 151.115(D)(3)(b) “Accessory Off-Street Parking
875Regulations” of the Zoning Ordinance (see Exhibits 1 & 2).

Lines 876–900

876 Currently, the Zoning Ordinance states, “… Parking areas and driveways surfaced with recycled
877 concrete and gravel are permitted in GI: General Industrial District when used outside of the
878 required building setbacks.”
879 The applicant’s proposed request would extend a similar allowance to the RI: Restrictive
880 Industrial District while recognizing the more restrictive nature of the district by incorporating
881 additional conditions (see Exhibit 2).
882 Based on the applicant’s request, gravel-surfaced parking areas and driveways within the RI
883 District would be required to be located outside all required building setbacks, maintain a
884 minimum separation of 200 feet from any residentially zoning property (measuring from the
885 nearest property line to the nearest property line), and occupy no more than 50 percent of total
886 area. Staff has added more restrictions to the language within this section of the Zoning
887 Ordinance (see Exhibit 1, Attachment A). Gravel will be limited only for heavy equipment uses
888 with a maximum gravel lot coverage of 25% for RI and 50% for GI.
889​
8903. The Town has reviewed two (2) pending Final Site Plans for Lots 7 & 8 of the Thompson
891Subdivision and both site plans included gravel in certain areas of the lot (see Exhibit 4).
892 The use of asphalt or concrete for outdoor display/storage of heavy equipment would create
893 excessive wear on the surfaces, causing them to be replaced more often. The use of gravel as
894 paving material does not cause the same issue.
895 Lot 7 is the Thompson Energy Solutions (TES) building, and it is approximately 293,167 square
896 feet. Approximately 46,989 square feet of gravel is proposed for this site, which is about 16% lot
897 coverage.
898 Lot 8 is the rental building and is approximately 236,181 square feet. Approximately 29,632
899 square feet of gravel is proposed for this site, which is about 12% lot coverage.
900 Both lots are zoned RI: Restrictive Industrial and if the applicant’s requested Zoning Text

Lines 901–925

901 Amendment were implemented to allow up to 50% of lot coverage, these lots could have up to
902 146,583 square feet (Lot 7) and 118,090 square feet (Lot 8) of gravel as a surface
903 area. Restricting lot coverage to 25% for RI zoned lots would be meet the goals of the zoning
904 ordinance by providing space for specific industrial uses, conform to a high level of performance
905 standards and have the least objectionable characteristics. Adding a limitation of up to 50% for
906 the GI zoning district also promotes the goal of the Town’s industrials zoning districts.

908STAFF RECOMMENDATION: Staff recommends approval of the Zoning Text Amendment
909(Ordinance 2026-10) as the request is warranted for uses with heavy equipment. The limitations on the
910location, use, and percentage of lot coverage will ensure off-site impacts are minimized.
