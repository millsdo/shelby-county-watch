12151Operating and Capital Expenditure Budgeting Process
12152​
12153 MLGW develops the operating and capital expenditure budgets annually to coincide with its Fiscal
12154Year, which extends from January 1 to December 31. The operating and capital budgets are developed
12155simultaneously and are presented together in a combined budget. In this manner, MLGW can accomplish
12156personnel planning and allocate resources to ensure the achievement of each strategic organization of focus.
12157Each annual budget is prepared in compliance with Memphis City Ordinance #3509, which requires
12158conformity with the National Association of Regulatory Utility Commissioners (NARUC) Uniform Systems
12159of Accounts, the regulations of any other applicable regulatory body, and in accordance with the provisions of
12160and the bond resolutions approved by the City Council. The budget process begins early in the year that
12161precedes the Fiscal Year for which the budget will take effect. The budget process continues for several
12162months until final approval is obtained by the City Council. At the onset of the budget process, a budget
12163schedule is created to establish the critical deadlines for activities necessary in producing the final approved
12164budget document. Some of the budget calendar events include planning meetings, multiple budget training
12165sessions, capital project budget meetings and equipment budget meetings, all of which are completed before
12166the end of June. During July through August, requests for the upcoming budget year are submitted by
12167organization supervisors and managers. The vice presidents are thereafter responsible for reviewing the
12168proposed budget and making necessary adjustments. The proposed budget goes through several stages of
12169formal review and approval before being finalized. The initial level of approval occurs during mid-August
12170through early October by the members of MLGW’s executive staff. A preliminary budget document is
12171subsequently developed and presented to the MLGW Board for review and approval in October with the final
12172budget approval by the City Council scheduled in November or December.
