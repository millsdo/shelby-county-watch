10552 Monthly service charges for gas services are generally based, in each case, upon a rate schedule
10553consisting of a minimum charge and a quantity charge. The minimum charges vary according to meter size
10554and account class, (e.g., residential, nonresidential/commercial). MLGW gas rate schedules are developed
10555using a projected price of natural gas and related gas storage and transportation charges. Retail natural gas
10556rates are adjusted monthly for the Purchased Gas Adjustment rider. A Purchased Gas Adjustment rider is
10557applied to customer bills to reflect the difference between the actual cost of gas, storage and transportation in
10558a given month and the projected levels built into the base rate schedule.

10563Gas Division on a self-supporting and financially sound basis, including requirements for interest and
10564principal payments on indebtedness, based on terms defined in the Rate Covenant, the City and the Board
10565shall put into effect promptly such changes in rates and charges as will provide the increased Gas Revenues
10566necessary to place the Gas Division upon a self-supporting and financially sound basis.
