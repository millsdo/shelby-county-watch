25228​
25229 MLGW develops the operating and capital expenditure budgets annually to coincide with its Fiscal
25230Year, which extends from January 1 to December 31. The operating and capital budgets are developed
25231simultaneously and are presented together in a combined budget. In this manner, MLGW can accomplish
25232personnel planning and allocate resources to ensure the achievement of each strategic organization of focus.
25233Each annual budget is prepared in compliance with Memphis City Ordinance #3509, which requires
25234conformity with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) Uniform Systems of Accounts, the
25235regulations of any other applicable regulatory body, and in accordance with the provisions of and the bond
25236resolutions approved by the City Council. The budget process begins early in the year that precedes the Fiscal
25237Year for which the budget will take effect. The budget process continues for several months until final
25238approval is obtained by the City Council. At the onset of the budget process, a budget schedule is created to
25239establish the critical deadlines for activities necessary in producing the final approved budget document.
25240Some of the budget calendar events include planning meetings, multiple budget training sessions, capital
25241project budget meetings and equipment budget meetings, all of which are completed before the end of June.
25242During July through August, requests for the upcoming budget year are submitted by organization supervisors
25243and managers. The vice presidents are thereafter responsible for reviewing the proposed budget and making
25244necessary adjustments. The proposed budget goes through several stages of formal review and approval
25245before being finalized. The initial level of approval occurs during mid-August through early October by the
25246members of MLGW’s executive staff. A preliminary budget document is subsequently developed and

25491consisting of a minimum charge and a quantity charge. The minimum charges vary according to meter size
25492and account class, (e.g., residential, nonresidential/commercial). MLGW gas rate schedules are developed
25493using a projected price of natural gas and related gas storage and transportation charges. Retail natural gas
25494rates are adjusted monthly for the Purchased Gas Adjustment rider. A Purchased Gas Adjustment rider is
25495applied to customer bills to reflect the difference between the actual cost of gas, storage and transportation in
25496a given month and the projected levels built into the base rate schedule.
