27361 Water utilities often apply declining block rate structures to commercial and industrial customers
27362because these customers typically consume water at a lower average cost to serve than residential users, and
27363the structure helps utilities recover costs while reflecting economies of scale. Declining block rates mean that
27364the more water a business uses, the cheaper each gallon becomes. Water utilities use this pricing model for
27365commercial customers because it is cheaper and easier to supply water in bulk to one large factory than to
27366hundreds of individual homes.
