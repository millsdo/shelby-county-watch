14133 The Memphis-Shelby County Airport Authority (the “Airport Authority”) was created in 1969

14139approximately 220 general aviation aircraft based at the Charles W. Baker Airport and the General DeWitt
14140Spain Airport. Both airports have automated fueling systems, aircraft maintenance services, hangars and tie
14141downs, aircraft rentals and charter services for general aviation. Runway 18-36 at Charles W. Baker Airport is
141423,500 feet in length and Runway 16-34 at the General DeWitt Spain Airport is 3,800 feet in length. Both
14143runways are 75 feet wide with asphalt surfaces.
