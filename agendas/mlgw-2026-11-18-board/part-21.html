8076Pension Plan Reform
8077​
8078 The Tennessee “Public Employee Defined Benefit Financial Security Act of 2014” (the “Public
8079Employee Defined Benefit Act”) was signed into law on May 22, 2014, and was codified as Title 9, Chapter
80803, Part 5, Tennessee Code Annotated. In order to improve financial security of public defined benefit plans,
8081the Public Employee Defined Benefit Act, among other things, requires each political subdivision which has
8082established and maintains, directly or indirectly, a defined benefit pension plan for the benefit of its
8083employees not administered by the Tennessee Consolidated Retirement System to: (a) adopt a resolution
8084delineating a funding policy for financing the obligations under the pension plan for fiscal years beginning
8085after June 15, 2015 (the “Funding Policy Resolution”); (b) utilize the level dollar amortization method for
8086financing the unfunded accrued liability beginning no later than the plan fiscal year commencing after June
808715, 2020; and (c) annually make a payment to the pension plan of no less than 100% of the actuarially
8088determined annual required contribution that incorporates both the normal cost of benefits and the
8089amortization of the pension plan's unfunded accrued liability (the “ADC”), provided however, the affected
8090political subdivision may make a payment of more than 100% of the ADC. If the political subdivision fails to
8091fund the ADC, the Public Employee Defined Benefit Act permits the State Commissioner of the Department
8092of Finance and Administration, at the direction of the Comptroller of the Treasury, to withhold such amount
8093or part of such amount from any state-shared taxes that are otherwise apportioned to such political
8094subdivision.
