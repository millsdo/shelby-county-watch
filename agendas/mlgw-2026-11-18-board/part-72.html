28485 The City of Memphis, Tennessee (the “City”), incorporated as a city in 1826, is a municipal
28486corporation organized under the laws of the State of Tennessee (the “State”). The City is located on the east
28487bank of the Mississippi River in the southwest corner of the State. The City is the State’s largest city not
28488having a metropolitan form of government and is the county seat of Shelby County, Tennessee. The City
28489currently occupies a land area of approximately 301 square miles and had an estimated population of
28490609,647 as of 2025, according to the United States Census Bureau.
28491​
28492 The City operated under a commission form of government from 1909 until January 1, 1968. At that
28493time through a charter, a mayor-council form of government was established. The Mayor of the City (the

28495and executive duties concerning day‐to‐day operations of the City government are the duty and responsibility
28496of the City Mayor. The City Mayor carries out the policies of the City and, with City Council approval,
28497appoints various City board members, officers and division directors. The City provides a full range of
28498municipal services including (i) public safety, (ii) culture/recreation, (iii) community development, (iv) solid
28499waste management, (v) public works, (vi) planning and zoning, (vii) sewers, (viii) utilities, (ix) transit, and
