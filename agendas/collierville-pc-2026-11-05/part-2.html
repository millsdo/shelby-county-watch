551 TDOTs Highway Systems Access Manual (HSAM) establish minimum driveway spacing
552 requirements. Using these requirements, the minimum driveway spacing required is 330 feet.
553 The HSAM does describe an exception to this spacing minimum if a lot's frontage length cannot
554 accommodate this spacing, but an access drive is still required. In this instance the minimum
555 driveway spacing shall be no less than the function area of the intersection.
556 The Green Book and HSAM establish every intersection has both a physical area and a larger
557 functional area, and states directly driveways should not be located within the functional area of
558 an intersection.
559 The functional area is the approach distance required for a driver to change speeds to complete a
560 movement, such as slowing for a turn. The functional area is the sum of the distance traveled
561 during driver perception/reaction time plus the deceleration distance while the driver slows to
562 stop or turn plus the queue storage length.
563 The functional area for the Distribution Parkway and Winchester Boulevard intersection once
564 fully constructed will be 150 feet. The current site plan shows the driveway 75 feet from the
565 intersection.
