308INTRODUCTION:
309 The Reaves Firm (Christine Donhardt), representing current property owner Michael Allen
310 Lightman Estate (Mark Puckett) and future property owner Smith/Packett Med-Com, LLC (Winn
311 Bishop), is requesting approval of a Preliminary Site Plan for a 111,563-square foot building
312 containing 120 assisted living units on 6.174 acres located on the south side of Market Blvd
313 across from the Warwick Willow Lane and Green Oaks Lane intersections (see Exhibit 1).
314 The property is zoned R-1: Low Density Residential with a Planned Development (PD) overlay
315 (Oakmont PD).
316​
317BACKGROUND:
318 The Outline Plan for the 119.45-acre Oakmont PD was approved by the PC in 1994. That PD
319 stipulates permitted uses, bulk zoning requirements, and some design requirements. A Final Plat
320 for Phase 4 was approved in 1997 (see Exhibit 6), and the applicable Outline Plan conditions are
321 noted on that document. Medical Professional Office: MPO and Shopping Center Commercial:
322 SCC uses are permitted in Area 4 per the PD.
323 A drycleaner was constructed in 1998 on a portion of Phase 4 to the northeast of the subject
324 property.
325 In 2015, the Town installed a traffic signal at Market Boulevard and Civic Center Drive. In 2022,

Lines 326–350

326 the BMA entered into an agreement with the current property owner for the construction of a
327 private drive that connected the signalized intersection to the Lowes property through an
328 easement acquired by the Town in 2013 across property owned by Target.
329 In 2024, the Final Plat (Exhibit 7) for Phase 4 was recorded.
330​
331KEY POINTS:
3321. This single-phase development will include assisted living and memory care units uses within
333one building facing Market Boulevard.
334 Memory Care will be located in a separate, secure portion of the building and will include
335 dedicated dining, activity spaces, programming, and a fenced outdoor garden.
336 As a senior housing use within a PD, § 151.159(B) of the Zoning Ordinance applies to this
337 development. These provisions address building height, density, exterior building materials,
338 building setbacks, and architectural design unless there is a conflict with a previously approved
339 standard expressly listed in the PD Outline Plan.
340 The PD does not have maximum Floor Area Ratio (FAR); however, §151.159(B)(4) addresses
341 scale and §151.159(B)(9) sets a maximum density of 20 units per acre for a 3-story building (123
342 units maximum for a 6.174-acre site).
343 The applicant’s parking study states the senior care facility will require 42 parking spaces and 105
344 are provided. Since this is not an independent living facility, the number of residents who can
345 drive will be less than 5% per the applicant. Transportation services will be available to residents
346 for shopping, medical appointments, and other outings.
347​
3482. Developing the currently vacant property as an assisted living and memory care facility will
349have minimal peak hour traffic impacts on the surrounding roadway network (see Exhibit 4).
350While the anticipated traffic volumes will be low, maintaining adequate vehicular sight distance is

Lines 351–375

351important for this site.
352 The Traffic Generation Letter (Exhibit 4) provided by the applicant states less than 28 AM and 35
353 PM peak hour vehicle trips are expected from this use, which is considerably less than if the
354 property was developed for retail uses. The traffic generated was below the threshold to warrant a
355 full Traffic Impact Analysis (TIA).
356 The 2004 Planned Development allows for three (3) access points to Market Boulevard. The
357 proposed site layout shows three (3) full access points. Two (2) access points already exist to
358 Market Boulevard, with one being a shared access point with the existing drycleaner aligned with
359 Green Oaks Drive and the other being at the signalized Market Boulevard/Civic Center
360 Drive/private drive intersection. The third access point is designed to be a full-movement
361 driveway and is aligned with Warwick Willow Lane to the north.
362 Because Market Boulevard curves along the site frontage, sight distance from the driveway is
