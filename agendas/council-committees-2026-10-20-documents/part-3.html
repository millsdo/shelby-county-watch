953Subject: Request for Legislative Assistance to Establish a Data Center Community Benefit
954Agency and Authorize Community Investment Funding
955​
956Dear Members of the Shelby County Legislative Delegation:
957The City of Memphis respectfully requests your assistance in developing and sponsoring
958Tennessee legislation that would authorize Memphis and other eligible municipalities to
959establish or designate a Data Center Community Benefit Agency.
960The purpose of this proposal is to establish a lawful, transparent, and accountable mechanism
961through which qualifying data center development can contribute to identified public needs,
962including infrastructure improvements, youth employment, after-school programming, housing
963rehabilitation, affordable housing, parks, and neighborhood revitalization.
964Memphis is evaluating regulatory measures governing the development and operation of large-
965scale data centers. The City Council’s proposed Ordinance No. 5986, sponsored by Councilman
966Jeff Warren, addresses data center siting and operational standards, including water
967conservation, cooling systems, electricity sources, public disclosure, and enforcement.
968We believe a complementary community-benefit funding framework merits consideration. Its
969purpose would be to address community priorities through an authorized funding mechanism
970that is separate from the City’s regulatory standards and from costs that data center operators
971must bear under applicable utility and infrastructure laws.
972​
973Requested Legislative Action
974We respectfully request that the Tennessee General Assembly consider legislation expressly
975authorizing eligible municipalities to establish or designate a Data Center Community Benefit

981demand at eligible data centers, with the applicable threshold, rate, measurement method,

985​
