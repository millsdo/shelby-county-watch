563The purpose of this Ordinance is to establish a municipal framework for developing a
564nonprofit Community Benefit Agency to facilitate the lawful receipt, administration,
565management, and distribution of eligible community-benefit funding associated with data
566centers operating or proposed to operate within the City of Memphis.

575functions;
