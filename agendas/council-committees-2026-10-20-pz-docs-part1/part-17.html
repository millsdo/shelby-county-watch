6403Analysis
6404The applicant is requesting to rename Ikea Way to Meetinghouse Way. The existing street name was established
6405to reflect the presence of the IKEA retail store. With the recent closure of the IKEA store, the existing street
6406name no longer accurately represents the identity or character of the area.
6407​
6408Staff finds that the proposed street name change is appropriate because it removes a street name that is directly
6409associated with a former business no longer operating at the site. Renaming the street to Meetinghouse Way
6410will provide a more relevant and enduring identity for the corridor that reflects the site's current and anticipated
6411future use. The proposed name also improves wayfinding by reducing potential confusion for visitors, delivery
6412services, and emergency responders who may otherwise associate the street with a business that no longer
6413exists.
6414​
6415The proposed street name change is expected to have minimal impacts on addressing. Only five properties are
6416currently addressed along this section of Ikea Way. Based on the foregoing, staff finds the proposed street name
6417change to be reasonable and consistent with the intent of maintaining an accurate and identifiable street
6418naming system.
6419​
6420RECOMMENDATION
6421​
6422Staff recommends approval with conditions.
6423​
6424Conditions
64251. The street name change shall take effect sixty (60) days after approval by the Memphis City Council.

6778Subject: Request for Roadway Renaming from “Ikea Way” to “Market Plaza Drive”
6779​
6780Dear Chair May Sharp and Members of the Land Use Control Board,
6781​
6782I am writing to formally request approval for the renaming of the roadway currently
6783designated as Ikea Way to its former name, Market Plaza Drive, located in Memphis,
6784Tennessee.
6785​
6786This request is being submitted as part of the recent closure and ongoing transition of the
6787property associated with this roadway. The name “Ikea Way” was originally established to
6788reﬂect the presence of the IKEA retail establishment at this location. As that use has since
6789ceased, the continuation of the IKEA-branded roadway no longer accurately represents the
6790current or future identity of the site.
6791​
6792The proposed renaming serves two primary purposes:
6793​
6794 1. Removal of Obsolete Branding:
6795 The existing street name is directly tied to a former corporate occupant that no
6796 longer operates at the property. Maintaining this branding may create confusion for
6797 visitors, tenants, emergency services, and the broader community. Renaming the
6798 roadway will eliminate references to a prior use that is no longer relevant.
6799​
