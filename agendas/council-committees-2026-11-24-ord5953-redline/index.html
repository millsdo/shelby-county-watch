Executive session attachment posted after Sun Nov 22: full redline of Community Benefits Ordinance 5953 (Cooper-Sutton) adding 5C Data Center, 46.88 acres on the east side of Getwell Road, as Property No. 4. The ordinance sets aside an amount equal to 25% of City real and personal property tax revenue collected on AI infrastructure at listed sites for public purposes within five miles, with at least 1% for environmental and sustainability topics. Ord. 5991 was on first reading Nov 24; the result was not clearly reported (no minutes yet).
