192POLICY 1031: Recruitment and Selection of Superintendent
193​
194TWhen a vacancy occurs, the selection and appointment of a Superintendent is an exclusive
195function of the Board.1 The Board is responsible for finding the person it believes can most
196effectively translate into action the policies of the Board and the goals of the community and the
197professional staff.
198​
199The Board may employ a consultant to advise and assist the Board in the search and selection
200process. However, final selection shall rest with the Board after a thorough consideration of

Lines 201–225

201qualified applicants. Should a vacancy in the office of Superintendent occur during the
202Superintendent’s contract term, the Board shall appoint an interim.2 An interim Superintendent
203appointed during the time of a searchrecruitment period shall not become a candidate unless the
204Board expressly permits such inclusion in the selection search procedures. A board member may
205not apply for or in any other way be considered for the position of the Superintendent.3
206​
207If the Board chooses to conduct an executive search to fill the Superintendent position, the
208Board, in cooperation with any consultant, shall initially develop the following:

210 1. A job description

212 2. A timeline

218 A. The Board may invite the community, including Board employees, to participate in
219 the process of selecting a Superintendent. Resumes of persons interviewed by the
220 Board shall be available for public inspection.
221 A.B. Resumes of persons considered and interviewed by the Board shall be treated as
222 confidential, and such materials shall not be open to public inspection.5
223 B.C. The interview process for each candidate selected for interview finalist shall
224 include an interview with the entire Board.
