553Site Plan Review
554The site plan shows the redevelopment of an approximately 1.11-acre (48,400-square-foot) property located at
5554628 Summer Avenue. The property is currently developed with a vacant restaurant building and is zoned CMU-
5561 (Commercial Mixed Use). The site is located at the northwest corner of Summer Avenue and Stratford Road
557and has frontage along both public streets. The survey identifies existing pedestrian easements, traffic signal
558easements, utilities, and rights-of-way affecting the property.
559​
560The site plan shows the construction of a new 4,284-square-foot one-story car wash building. The proposed
561development includes a wash tunnel, customer parking, 16 vacuum stalls, one ADA parking space, internal drive
562aisles, sidewalks, vacuum enclosure structures, lighting, utility improvements, and associated site infrastructure.
563Vehicular circulation is designed as a one-way pattern through the site, with customer vehicles entering from
564Stratford Road, proceeding through the wash tunnel and vacuum area, and exiting onto Summer Avenue. The
565existing driveway on Summer Avenue will be closed and the curb and sidewalk restored. The existing sign base
566along Summer Avenue is proposed to remain and be reused for an updated sign.
567​
568The site plan provides perimeter landscape areas surrounding the development, including landscape strips
569adjacent to Summer Avenue, Stratford Road, and the northern property line. A 6-foot-tall white vinyl fence is
570proposed along the northern property line to provide screening between the commercial development and the
571residential property. An increased landscape buffer between the proposed car wash and the adjacent residential
572properties in accordance is required.

575Analysis

Lines 576–600

576The applicant is requesting approval of a Special Use Permit to allow a car wash within the CMU-1 Commercial
577Mixed Use zoning district. The subject property was rezoned by the Memphis City Council through Ordinance
578No. 5773, adopted on February 2, 2021, from CMU-3 to CMU-1, reflecting the City's intent to reduce the
579intensity of development and encourage land uses that are more compatible with the surrounding area. While
580the property is located along the Summer Avenue commercial corridor, it directly abuts an established single-
581family residential neighborhood to the north.
582​
583The submitted site plan includes a one-story car wash building, customer parking, vacuum stations, internal
584drive aisles, sidewalks, landscaping, and architectural enhancements. However, the proposed layout does not
585adequately address the land use concerns associated with a vehicle-oriented use along this corridor. Unlike
586development patterns that provide a stronger street presence through building orientation, the proposed car
587wash places the building away from the street frontage and dedicates a substantial portion of the site to
588circulation, queuing, and vacuum operations rather than pedestrian-oriented commercial activity.
