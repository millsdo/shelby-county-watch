286REQUEST: Special use permit to allow a car wash
287EXISTING ZONING: Commercial Mixed Use – 1 (CMU-1) District
288CONCLUSIONS
2891. The applicant is requesting approval of a Special Use Permit to allow a car wash within the CMU-1 Commercial
290 Mixed Use zoning district. The subject property was rezoned by the Memphis City Council through Ordinance
291 No. 5773, adopted on February 2, 2021, from CMU-3 to CMU-1, reflecting the City's intent to reduce the
292 intensity of development and encourage land uses that are more compatible with the surrounding area. While
293 the property is located along the Summer Avenue commercial corridor, it directly abuts an established single-
294 family residential neighborhood to the north.
2952. The submitted site plan includes a one-story car wash building, customer parking, vacuum stations, internal
296 drive aisles, sidewalks, landscaping, and architectural enhancements. However, the proposed layout does not
297 adequately address the land use concerns associated with a vehicle-oriented use along this corridor. Unlike
298 development patterns that provide a stronger street presence through building orientation, the proposed car
299 wash places the building away from the street frontage and dedicates a substantial portion of the site to
300 circulation, queuing, and vacuum operations rather than pedestrian-oriented commercial activity.

Lines 301–325

3013. There are four existing car wash facilities located within the approximately five-mile segment of Summer
302 Avenue between North Parkway and North White Station Road, indicating that this commercial corridor is
303 already served by multiple similar uses.
3044. The proposed car wash would also be located in close proximity to existing single-family residences, directly
305 abutting a residential property line at the northwest corner of the property, the nearest single-family home
306 principal structure (duplex) being approximately 145 feet from the principal car wash building, and
307 approximately 185 feet from the nearest outdoor vacuum station. Given the inherently noisy nature of car wash
308 operations and outdoor vacuum stations, the limited separation between the proposed use and adjacent
309 residences is incompatible with the surrounding residential neighborhood.
3105. The granting of this special use permit will cause substantial detriment to the public good, it will substantially
311 impair the intent and purpose of an adopted plan or the Unified Development Code (UDC), it will be injurious
312 to the neighborhood and the general welfare, and it will not be in harmony with the purpose and intent of the
313 UDC.
314​

317This proposal is consistent with the Memphis 3.0 General Plan per the land use decision criteria. See further
318analysis on pages 20-22 of this report.
