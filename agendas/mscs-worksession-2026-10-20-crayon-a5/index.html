13Recommendation: It is recommended that the Shelby County Board of Education approve the contract with Crayon
14Software Experts LLC, an authorized Microsoft licensing reseller, in an amount not to exceed $2,365,230.74 for the purchase
15of Microsoft Office 365 A5 Education software licenses for the District.

17Purpose/Description and Background: The purpose of this purchase is for the District’s Microsoft Office 365 A5
18Software Licenses. Microsoft Office Education 365 A5 subscription includes Microsoft Office client apps (i.e., Word,
19PowerPoint, SharePoint, Teams, Publisher, Excel, Outlook, OneDrive, OneNote) Windows, advanced device, user, and security
20management, plus Advanced Threat Protection, Threat Intelligence, Advanced Compliance, MyAnalytics & Power BI Pro, and
21PSTN Conferencing & Cloud PBX, as well as Cloud Infrastructure Suite Datacenter and Visual Studio Enterprise MSDN for the
22Application and Technology Development teams.

30Measurable Outcomes Expected: Outcome 1 — Maintain 100% licensed coverage for 17,000 staff and 116,000 student
