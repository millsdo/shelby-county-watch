12INTRODUCTION:
13The purpose of this agenda item is to request approval from the Board of Mayor and Aldermen (BMA)
14of the First Amendment to the Professional Services Agreement with Fisher & Arnold, Inc. for
15Construction Engineering and Inspection (CEI) services associated with the sanitary sewer force main
16relocation for the TDOT Col-Arlington Wolf River Bridge Replacement Project.
17​
18BACKGROUND:
19April 4, 2017 – The Town of Collierville submitted a formal request to the Tennessee Department of
20Transportation (TDOT) requesting full replacement of the bridges over the Wolf River and Wolf River
21Overflow.
22​
23June 13, 2025 – Following notification that TDOT intended to move forward with the bridge
24replacement on SR-205 (Collierville-Arlington Road), the Town formally notified TDOT of the existing
25sanitary sewer force main located along the west side of the roadway within the proposed project limits.

46DISCUSSION:
47Fisher & Arnold’s original Professional Services Agreement provides engineering design services for
48relocation of the Town’s sanitary sewer force main along SR-205 within the limits of TDOT’s Wolf
49​
50River and Wolf River Overflow bridge replacement project.

Lines 51–75

51​
52As the project advances toward construction, Construction Engineering and Inspection services will be
53required to provide technical oversight of the force main relocation. The First Amendment adds Task B
54– Construction Engineering and Inspection to Fisher & Arnold’s existing agreement.
55​
56The CEI scope includes responding to bidder questions and issuing clarifications or addenda as
57required; attending the preconstruction meeting; reviewing material submittals; attending periodic
58construction progress meetings; providing a full-time Quality Control Technician to monitor relocation,
59testing, and commissioning of the new force main; and preparing record drawings following completion
60of the relocation.
61​
62Fisher & Arnold proposed providing these services on an hourly basis with an estimated ceiling of
63$75,889.78. The First Amendment therefore increases the existing contract amount by $75,889.78, from
64$23,582.62 to a total amended contract amount of $99,472.40.
65​
66The CEI services are necessary to provide construction oversight and documentation for the Town’s
67sanitary sewer force main relocation while coordinating the work with TDOT’s bridge replacement
68project.
