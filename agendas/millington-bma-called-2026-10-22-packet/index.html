127PURPOSE
128The purpose of this Agreement is to outline the way the Parties will cooperate regarding implementation of
129micro-transit service within the City of Millington (City).
130​
131This agreement constitutes the City Council of the City of Millington, Tennessee approving an interlocal
132agreement with Delta Human Resource Agency (DHRA) that: 1) Provides a process and framework
133under which the City and DHRA will coordinate micro-transit mode of rural public transit services within
134the City; 2) Establishes the initial transit projects; 3) Establishes the local match funding agreement
135whereas the City will provide local match to support the services under the DHRA Section 5311 program;
136and 4) Establishes the project reporting criteria to support the agreed upon service.
137​
138AGREEMENT
139This Micro-Transit Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective as of , 20
140 ("Effective Date"), by and between the City of Millington, Tennessee ("City"), and
141Delta Human Resource Agency ("DHRA"). The City and DHRA may each be referred to as a "Party"
142and collectively as the "Parties."

147of Millington;

166DHRA shall assign dedicated vehicle(s) for the operation of rural public transit micro-transit service
167within the incorporated limits of the City of Millington. This service enhancement to the existing DHRA

169members of the general public while meeting state and federal rural public transit requirements.
