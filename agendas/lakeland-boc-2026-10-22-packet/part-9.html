3465Policy
3466​
3467The City of Lakeland is responsible for maintaining the ROW for all public roadways that are
3468under Lakeland’s jurisdiction. To ensure integrity of the roadway the City currently requires
3469that any utility provider proposing to install their utility lines within this public ROW ﬁll out a
3470Road Cut Permit. The Lakeland Municipal Code, Title 16. Streets and Sidewalks, ETC,
3471Chapter 2. Road Cuts and Borings, Sections 16-301 to 16-210 clearly outline the
3472expectations regarding the application and performance of this type of work.
3473​
3474Before the City of Lakeland will authorize any work to be performed under this permit it is
3475mandatory the applicant schedule and attend a pre-construction meeting with Engineering

3476City Staff. During this meeting the applicant and staff discuss and agree upon the
3477construction schedule for the work. This includes, but is not limited to, start date, end date,
3478location, primary utility contact, and 24-hour utility contact. In addition, City staff verify the
3479applicant has provided or plans to provide prior notiﬁcation to residents directly affected
