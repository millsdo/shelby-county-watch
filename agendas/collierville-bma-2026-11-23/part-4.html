1360INTRODUCTION:
1361The purpose of this agenda item is to obtain Board of Mayor and Alderman approval of a contract for
1362the purchase of organic polymer for the Shelton Road Wastewater Treatment Plant and the Northwest
1363Wastewater Treatment Plant from CedarChem, LLC.
1364​
1365This will be a twelve (12) month contract with an option to extend for four additional twelve (12) month
1366terms, for a combined of forty-eight (48) additional months.
1367​
1368BACKGROUND:
1369The wastewater treatment plants remove solids from the system utilizing belt filter presses and screw
1370presses. The process requires an organic polymer to condition the solids that are fed into the presses
1371flocculating the solids so water can be removed from the sludge more efficiently when compacted. The
1372pressed solids are then transported for disposal to the Quad County landfill in Byhalia, MS.
1373​
1374DISCUSSION:
1375All potential vendors were required to submit samples for testing at each of the wastewater treatment

Lines 1376–1400

1376plants. The polymer trial evaluations rated the weight and efficiency of the polymer as well as cost for
1377disposal. To qualify, a polymer's performance had to meet minimum percent cake solids and percent
1378capture at a specified filter feed rate. The approved polymers were rated based on the overall annual
1379cost of operation, which included the cost of the polymer and cost of transportation and disposal of the
1380solids.
1381​
1382Polymer is a chemical where price per pound is not the sole determining factor. Staff utilize a
1383spreadsheet to calculate total monthly cost which includes the cost of the polymer and cost of
1384transportation and disposal. The polymer with the lowest total monthly cost is the polymer that is
1385selected. In the event of a tie, items such as solids infiltrate and cake solids are used for a tie breaker.
1386​
1387There were two (2) companies that submitted bids for the polymer contract, with all being considered
1388responsive bidders. Based on the factors listed above, CedarChem LLC. had the lowest combined cost
1389performance for both the Shelton Road Wastewater Treatment Plant and the Northwest Wastewater
1390Treatment Plant (see attached performance evaluation sheet).
1391​
1392The estimated annual cost for polymer is $71,448, based on the performance CedarChem, LLCs
1393polymer from the plant trials. The amount of polymer used annually is dependent on the quantity of
