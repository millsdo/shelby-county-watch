845sales, and specialty training. These four (4) applications were heard at the October 1, 2026, PC meeting
846for recommendation. There are also two (2) Final Site Plans pending for Lots 7 and 8. Staff has
847provided consolidated comments to the applicant and are awaiting revisions. The PC recommended
848approval of Ordinance 2026-10 on October 1, 2026. The BMA approved Ordinance 2026-10 on First

Lines 851–875

851DISCUSSION:
8521. According to the Zoning Ordinance, industrial districts are designed to promote and protect
853the health, safety, morals, convenience, order, prosperity and other aspects of the general welfare.
854 The Town has two (2) types of industrial zoning district (GI: General Industrial & RI: Restrictive
855 Industrial). The RI zoning district is designed to provide space for a wide range of industrial and
856 related uses conforming to a high level of performance standards and have the least objectionable
857 characteristics. RI zoning district is used as a buffer between other districts and other industrial
858 activities having more objectionable influences.
859 All operations within the RI zoning district must be carried on within completely enclosed
860 buildings. This prevents any adverse characteristics from affecting neighboring properties.
861 Exceptions could include customary outdoor display of heavy equipment for sale or rent.
862 The GI zoning district is designed to provide space for a wide range of industrial and related uses
863 which by reasons of volume of raw materials or freight, scale of operation, type of structures
864​
865 required or other similar characteristics require locations relatively well segregated from
866 nonindustrial uses. Outdoor activity is common in GI districts.
867​
8682. The applicant has requested to amend section 151.115(D)(3)(b) “Accessory Off-Street Parking
869Regulations” of the Zoning Ordinance (see Exhibits 1 & 2).
870 Currently, the Zoning Ordinance states, “… Parking areas and driveways surfaced with recycled
871 concrete and gravel are permitted in GI: General Industrial District when used outside of the
872 required building setbacks.”
873 The applicant’s proposed request would extend a similar allowance to the RI: Restrictive
874 Industrial District while recognizing the more restrictive nature of the district by incorporating
875 additional conditions (see Exhibit 2).

Lines 876–900

876 Based on the applicant’s request, gravel-surfaced parking areas and driveways within the RI
877 District would be required to be located outside all required building setbacks, maintain a
878 minimum separation of 200 feet from any residentially zoning property (measuring from the
879 nearest property line to the nearest property line), and occupy no more than 50 percent of total
880 area. Staff has added more restrictions to the language within this section of the Zoning
881 Ordinance (see Exhibit 1, Attachment A). Gravel will be limited only for heavy equipment uses
882 with a maximum gravel lot coverage of 25% for RI and 50% for GI.
883​
8843. The Town has reviewed two (2) pending Final Site Plans for Lots 7 & 8 of the Thompson
885Subdivision and both site plans included gravel in certain areas of the lot (see Exhibit 4).
886 The use of asphalt or concrete for outdoor display/storage of heavy equipment would create
887 excessive wear on the surfaces, causing them to be replaced more often. The use of gravel as
888 paving material does not cause the same issue.
889 Lot 7 is the Thompson Energy Solutions (TES) building, and it is approximately 293,167 square
890 feet. Approximately 46,989 square feet of gravel is proposed for this site, covering about 16%.
891 Lot 8 is the rental building and is approximately 236,181 square feet. Approximately 29,632
892 square feet of gravel is proposed for this site, covering about 12%.
893 Both lots are zoned RI: Restrictive Industrial and if the applicant’s requested Zoning Text
894 Amendment were implemented to allow up to 50% of lot coverage, these lots could have up to
895 146,583 square feet (Lot 7) and 118,090 square feet (Lot 8) of gravel as a surface
896 area. Restricting lot coverage to 25% for RI zoned lots would be meet the goals of the zoning
897 ordinance by providing space for specific industrial uses, conform to a high level of performance
898 standards and have the least objectionable characteristics. Adding a limitation of up to 50% for
899 the GI zoning district also promotes the goal of the Town’s industrials zoning districts.
