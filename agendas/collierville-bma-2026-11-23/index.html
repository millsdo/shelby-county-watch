330The first amendment, Resolution 2006-53 was approved at the November 27, 2006, BMA meeting. The
331resolution was established to provide reimbursement for qualifying sewer improvements and related
332eligible expenses. Since implementation, the policy has provided an important infrastructure needs for
333the southeast side of the Town. Examples are Collierville High School, Meadows of Clara’s Ridge, The
334Grove of Clara’s Ridge and Fire Station 6.
335​
336Over the past 20 years, this area of the Town has experienced growth, with a significant portion
337occurring within the Rolling Meadows Development. As the original developer of Rolling Meadows,
338the development is not subject to the sewer reimbursement fee. Extending the existing resolution would
339provide the Developer with additional time to collect applicable reimbursement fees from future
340development within the service area. These fees would assist in offsetting the Developer’s costs
341associated with the installation and construction of the sewer line and help ensure the financial burden
342of the infrastructure investment is appropriately distributed among benefiting properties. The payments
343are collected yearly from January to December, and the payments are to be made to the Developer no
344later than February of the following year.
345​
346DISCUSSION:
347Meadows Estate, LLC is requesting a 10-year extension to the existing agreement. The proposed
348amendment would also waive any reimbursement fees owed to Meadows Estates, LLC for Town
349projects connecting to the sewer system.
