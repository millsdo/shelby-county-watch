401 Providing for the Disposal of Such Property
402​
403INTRODUCTION:
404The purpose of this agenda item is to request approval from the Board of Mayor and Aldermen to
405declare certain seized vehicles as surplus.
406​
407BACKGROUND:
408Individual items valued at $10,000 or more, along with all computers and portable radios, must be
409added to the Town’s fixed asset inventory and given an asset tag. This requirement also applies to items
410purchased before July 1, 2010 valued at $500 or more; before July 1, 2014 valued at $1,000 or more;
411and before December 12, 2022 valued at $5,000 or more. The Finance Department issues asset tags and
412tracks each item for inventory and depreciation. An asset stays on the inventory list until the department
413determines it is obsolete or has reached the end of its useful life. At that point, the item must be
414formally surplused and disposed of according to Town policy.
415​
416The surplus process begins when a department decides an item should be surplused and reviews basic
417questions, such as whether the item works, whether it is obsolete or broken, its fair market value,
418whether it can be sold, and whether another department or government entity has requested it. The
419department then submits a Surplus Request form with supporting documentation to the Finance
420Department, which processes the request and prepares to remove the asset from inventory. A resolution
421listing the items ready for surplus is prepared and sent to the Procurement Manager, who reports it to the
422Board of Mayor and Aldermen for approval. Once approved, staff dispose of the item through an online
423auction, recycling, donation, departmental transfer, or another method determined to be in the Town’s
424best interest.
425​

430of these items from the Town’s fixed asset database. The planned disposal method for each item is
431noted in the resolution, and all surplus equipment will be disposed of in the most advantageous manner.

465INTRODUCTION:
466The purpose of this Agenda item is to: approve the write-off of $11,187.22 of uncollectible personal

468total is $17,031.10. Please refer to Exhibit A for a detailed list of parcels included in the write-off.
469​
470BACKGROUND:
471Collection policies and procedures: Collierville sends property tax bill notices in November with a due
472date of January 1st of the subsequent year. Reminder notices are mailed to property owners in February
473and unpaid property taxes become delinquent on and after March 1st. A series of three delinquent
474notices and a final warning notice are issued prior to the tax account being sent to the Shelby County
475Clerk & Master for collection in May of the following year.

Lines 476–500

476​
477The County pursues collection on Collierville’s behalf utilizing its larger staff and legal resources. The
478County’s efforts continue until one of the following occurs: (1) payment is received; (2) the account
479meets criteria for write-off; or (3) the statute of limitation expires. Tax liabilities less than $5.00, those
480under appeal or bankruptcy, and those with written payment agreements are not sent to the Clerk &
481Master.
482​
483If the County deems real and/or personal property tax uncollectible, Town staff submits the total tax
484liability (including penalties) and associated parcels to the BMA for write-off as no further collection
485efforts will be made. Any delinquent personal property tax, less than twelve years old, is considered for
486write-off if research determines the business has closed (as confirmed by the County, Secretary of State,
487or the Town’s business license records). Any delinquent real property tax, less than twelve years old, is
488considered for write-off if the property has been acquired by any governmental entity. All delinquent
489taxes over twelve years old are considered for write-off. Should staff see a possibility of collection, the
490submission to the BMA for write-off is postponed for the related parcels.
491​

494related to businesses that have closed and determined uncollectible by Shelby County Trustee.
