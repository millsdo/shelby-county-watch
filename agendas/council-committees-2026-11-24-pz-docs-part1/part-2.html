458 CONCLUSIONS
4591. The applicant is proposing Raleigh Gardens Planned Development, a single-family residential community on approximately
460 11.72 acres along Egypt-Central Road. The proposed development would establish a neighborhood of 34 single-family homes
461 with shared open spaces, a community center, and supporting infrastructure. The property is currently vacant and is zoned R-
462 8 and CMU-1. The proposed Planned Development would establish an Outline Plan based on the development standards of
463 the R-6 zoning district, allowing for coordinated residential development with smaller lots than those typically permitted under
464 the existing R-8 zoning.
4652. The surrounding area includes a mixture of residential, commercial, institutional, and undeveloped properties. Single-family
466 neighborhoods are located north of Egypt-Central Road, while institutional and commercial uses are concentrated farther west
467 near Raleigh Millington Road. The proposed development would provide a transition between these existing uses and the
468 larger undeveloped properties to the east and south. The proposed single-family homes would be consistent with the
469 residential character of the neighborhood, particularly the established subdivisions north of the subject property.
4703. The proposed layout also incorporates approximately 3.39 acres of common open space, representing nearly 29 percent of the
471 overall development. These areas would provide opportunities for passive recreation while accommodating shared facilities
472 and stormwater detention. The inclusion of a community center would provide an additional amenity for residents. The
473 proposed private street network would serve the residential lots internally, with access concentrated along Egypt-Central Road
474 rather than introducing individual driveways along the main roadway.
4754. The applicant has incorporated landscaping and buffering along the development frontage and near the proposed community

Lines 476–500

476 facilities. These improvements would help establish a consistent streetscape and provide separation between the residential
477 lots, shared amenities, and surrounding properties. The proposed three-phase development approach would allow residential
478 construction and associated infrastructure to proceed in stages, subject to the required reviews and approvals.
4795. Overall, staff finds the proposed Planned Development to be an appropriate use of the subject property. The project would
480 introduce additional single-family housing in an area that already includes established residential neighborhoods, while making
481 use of currently vacant land. The proposed layout, common open spaces, landscaping, and neighborhood amenities provide a
482 coordinated approach to development and help address compatibility with surrounding properties.
4836. The project will not have a substantial or undue adverse effect upon adjacent property, the character of the neighborhood,
484 traffic conditions, parking, utility facilities and other matters affecting the public health, safety, and general welfare.

7364.10.4 Planned Residential Developments
737In addition to the standards and criteria set forth in Section 4.10.3, planned residential developments shall
738comply with the standards and criteria set forth below:
739 A. Formal Open Space
740 A minimum of 0.6% of the total land area of a planned residential development of 15 acres or more
741 shall be subject to the formal open space requirements of Section 6.2.3. No open area may be
742 delineated or accepted as formal open space under the provisions of this Chapter unless it meets the
743 standards of Chapter 6.2, Open Space.
744 B. Accessibility of Site
745 All proposed streets, alleys and driveways shall be adequate to serve the residents, occupants, visitors
746 or other anticipated traffic of the planned residential development. The location of the entrance
747 points of the streets, alleys and driveways upon existing public roadways shall be subject to the
748 approval of the City or County Division of Public Works.
