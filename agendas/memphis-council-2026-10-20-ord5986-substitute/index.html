259SECTION 10. TEMPORARY ONE HUNDRED THIRTY-FIVE (135) DAY PAUSE ON NEW
260DATA-CENTER APPLICATIONS AND DEVELOPMENT APPROVALS
261A. Purpose
262To preserve the effectiveness of the permanent land-use standards required by this Ordinance
263while DPD completes the expedited analysis and drafting process, a temporary pause is hereby
264established on new applications and specified City approvals for the establishment or
265construction of new data centers.
266The pause is intended solely to provide a limited period for development of permanent standards
267and shall not be interpreted as a permanent prohibition upon data-center development within the
268City of Memphis.
269B. Duration
270The temporary pause shall commence upon the effective date of this Ordinance and shall
271terminate at 11:59 p.m. on the one-hundred-thirty-fifth (135th) calendar day thereafter, or earlier
272upon the date comprehensive permanent data-center land-use and siting ordinances are lawfully
273adopted and become effective and enforceable, whichever first occurs.
274The pause shall not be administratively extended beyond the 135-day period.
275Any extension shall require separate legislative action by the Memphis City Council in

Lines 276–300

276accordance with applicable law.
277C. New Applications, Review, Hearings, and Covered Approvals
278During the 135-day temporary pause, no City department, division, board, commission, officer,
279or employee shall accept for formal filing, process, schedule for hearing, hear, approve, issue, or
280grant any new application, permit, or City land-use or development approval authorizing the
281establishment or construction of a new Data Center where such approval is within the regulatory
282jurisdiction of the City of Memphis.
283Covered applications and approvals include, to the extent lawfully subject to this temporary
284pause:
2851. applications for zoning changes or other land-use approvals for a covered facility;
2862. applications for Special Use Permits or other discretionary land-use approvals for a covered
287facility;
2883. applications for site-plan, development-plan, grading, or other City development approvals for
289a covered facility;
2904. applications for building permits authorizing construction of a covered facility; and
2915. other City development applications or approvals determined by the Law Division to be
292legally subject to the temporary pause.
