Memphis: $3,259,282 data center revenue plan approved; two ordinances held to Dec 22 (new since Sunday)

MemphisMemphis City CouncilTue Nov 10, 4:00 PM (held)

“30. RESOLUTION to Allocate and Appropriate ... ($3,259,282.00) in Data Center Revenues Collected During FY2026 to Nine Projects and Programs, in accordance with Community Benefit Ordinance Number 5953.”
Agenda
Details
  • Approved with 12 yes votes read aloud and no one heard voting no (Memphis Report, Nov 10). The money is the 25% set-aside of City property taxes paid by the AI property, which shows the site paying real taxes. Home repairs get $2,000,000 (the "HOPE grant," per the City's chief operating officer). The approved version no longer has the August draft's $100,000 environmental monitoring line.
  • Held to Tue Dec 22: Ord. 5987 (data center zoning amendments, item 23) and Ord. 5985 (large-load district program replacing the blanket moratorium, item 25; already held Oct 6 and Oct 20). No reason was given on the livestream (news report).
  • Ord. 5988 (siting framework, item 24) got no vote. Members said the Council had already passed it, and the chair called its listing "an oversight." Memphis Report found no official record yet of its third-reading adoption, so that is unconfirmed.
  • Also approved: Cooper-Sutton's resolution requesting comprehensive data center standards. Ord. 5989 (nonprofit "community benefit agency") passed second reading on consent; it needs a third reading.
  • Councilman Spinosa said the Monday data center work sessions will continue before each Tuesday Council meeting (news report). The next full Council is Tue Nov 24, 4:00 PM.
