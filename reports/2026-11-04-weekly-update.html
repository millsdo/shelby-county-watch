What changed since Sunday (Nov 1, about 6:15 PM CT): Collierville posted its Mon Nov 9 Board of Mayor and Aldermen agenda. Ordinance 2026-12 (second reading) adds "Data Center" to the town's zoning code as a use allowed only by conditional use permit in industrial zones, and staff may add data center permit standards at the second or third reading (Nov 23). A joint, non-voting work session of the Board and the Planning Commission on Nov 9 will discuss it. Lakeland posted its Thu Nov 5 Board of Commissioners agenda: a $49,599.21 emergency generator purchase for Fire Station #1 that was already ordered before the vote, a re-approved development contract for the Estates at Chambers Chapel, and a code recodification. The Lakeland Beer Board (Thu Nov 5) and the Germantown Board of Zoning Appeals (Tue Nov 10) also posted agendas. The Memphis Council's Public Safety committee moved from Election Day to Fri Nov 6, 10:45 AM, and the Public Works committee is now Nov 17. Election results (unofficial): Memphis voters passed all three city charter amendments (library workers into civil service, civil service appeals, lobbyist registration); Germantown elected Tony Salvaggio mayor; Lakeland re-elected Mayor Josh Roman; two Collierville alderman seats go to December runoffs. No County Commission news: no new agenda, meeting, or action since Sunday; committees meet Nov 18 and the Commission Nov 23. Arlington was re-checked in a browser this morning: nothing new.

Collierville: Ordinance 2026-12 adds "Data Center" as a permitted-by-CUP industrial use (new since Sunday)

ColliervilleCollierville Board of Mayor and AldermenMon Nov 9, 6:00 PM

“Adds the use “Data Center” to the Zoning Ordinance and only allow them via the Conditional Use Permit (CUP) process in industrial areas.”
Show full quotes
“Adds the use “Data Center” to the Zoning Ordinance and only allow them via the Conditional Use Permit (CUP) process in industrial areas.”
“A joint non-voting work session of the BMA and PC is set for November 9, 2026, to discuss public notice for administrative cases and potentially adding data center standards to Ordinance 2026-12 prior to adoption.”
Agenda
Details
  • Item 10.D, second reading and public hearing. The ordinance defines a data center as an establishment that typically requires "industrial-scale power, cooling, or infrastructure," and keeps ordinary office "Data Processing" allowed by right. Staff "is researching model ordinances" and may add data center CUP provisions to §151.024 at the second or third reading. The third and final reading is set for Mon Nov 23.
  • The ordinance is mostly a state-law cleanup (the new development "shot clock" law, effective Jan 1, 2027). The Planning Commission recommended it Oct 1. Online comments on SpeakUp close Mon Nov 9 at 9:00 AM. The work session time is not posted.
