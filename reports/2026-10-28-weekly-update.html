County defers its data center moratorium and data center standards (new since Sunday)

CountyShelby County Board of CommissionersMon Oct 26, 3:00 PM

“ORDINANCE – SECOND READING – Ordinance declaring a Temporary Moratorium, not to exceed twelve (12) months, on “permits” … for Data Center developments”
Agenda
Details
  • News reports: the Commission put off item 28 (standards, Huett-Garcia) and item 29 (12-month moratorium, Brooks and Sugarmon) without a vote. The Commercial Appeal reports that seven more commissioners co-sponsored the standards ordinance by the end of the meeting. It also reports that Brooks plans amendments to the moratorium "to ensure the county is staying in its legal lane." ABC24 says both items go to "the Commission's Nov. 18 meeting." Nov 18 is a Wednesday, the usual committee day, and the agenda adjourned the full Commission to Mon Nov 23. Treat the date as unconfirmed.
  • The amended standards text ("AMENDED IN OCTOBER 21, 2026 COMMITTEE MEETING") is still not posted.
  • Context: data centers add to the County's tax base. The City set aside $3,259,282 in FY2026 data center revenue, its 25% share of City property taxes from one AI property on Paul R. Lowery Rd.
