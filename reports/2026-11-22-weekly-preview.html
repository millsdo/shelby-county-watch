Shelby County Weekly Meeting Preview: Nov 23 - 29, 2026

Red flags are leads for review, not accusations.

Thanksgiving week, with two big days up front. Mon Nov 23: the County Commission (3:00 PM) takes up the data center standards ordinance on first reading (substituted and amended in committee Nov 18; needs a two-thirds vote and Land Use Control Board approval; the data center moratorium is not on the agenda), the household hazardous waste agreements with Arlington and the other towns (third reading), the Collierville-Arlington Road bridge right-of-way, and Commissioner Derek Mills's resolution to start Commission meetings at 1:00 PM (sent down with an unfavorable recommendation). Collierville votes the same night on third reading of Ord. 2026-12, which adds a data center use. Tue Nov 24: the Memphis Council's MLGW committee takes up gas rate increases of 12%, 11% and 11% and water increases of 16% a year for 2027-2029, plus new data center water tariffs. The official documents conflict on whether the Council holds the rate public hearing that day (see Watch list). The full Council has Ord. 5991 (a fourth data center property in the community benefits ordinance) on first reading and Ord. 5989 (data center community benefit agency) on third and final reading. Arlington's own boards do not meet this week; the next Board of Mayor and Aldermen meeting is Mon Dec 7. No school board meets this week.

Data centers: County standards ordinance, first reading

CountyShelby County Board of CommissionersMon Nov 23, 3:00 PM

“ORDINANCE - FIRST READING –Ordinance amending Ordinance No. 397, known as the Memphis and Shelby County Unified Development Code, to establish regulations for the siting ... of large-scale Data Centers ... (SUBSTITUTED AND…”
Show full quote
“ORDINANCE - FIRST READING –Ordinance amending Ordinance No. 397, known as the Memphis and Shelby County Unified Development Code, to establish regulations for the siting ... of large-scale Data Centers ... (SUBSTITUTED AND AMENDED IN NOVEMBER 18, 2026 COMMITTEE MEETING)”
Agenda
Details
  • Item 44. Sponsors: Commissioners Huett-Garcia, Caswell, Richardson, Streeter, Rudd, Clay-Bibbs, Sugarmon, Brooks and Chairwoman Avant. The packet says it "requires a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the membership" and prior Land Use Control Board approval.
  • It covers sites with "a projected electrical demand of twenty (20) megawatts or greater," requires closed-loop cooling, and phases in renewable or non-carbon power. The substitute adds size tiers up to "Data Center, Hyperscale (at least 100MW)."
  • The text says that if the pending moratorium passes, "this ordinance will replace it." The moratorium itself is not on the Nov 23 agenda. Second reading would likely come Dec 14. New data center investment adds to the County's tax base.

MLGW: gas and water rate increases for 2027-2029 at Council committee; hearing date conflicts

MemphisMemphis City Council MLGW committeeTue Nov 24, 10:35 AM

“A resolution to approve the 2027 increase in gas revenues of 12.0% ... the 2028 increase in gas revenues of 11.0% ... and the 2029 increase in gas revenues of 11.0%”
Agenda
Details
  • Water: "the 2027 increase in water revenues of 16.0%," and 16.0% again in 2028 and 2029, plus the new "Water Division W-15 and W-16 Data Center Rate Schedules." Also a new electric vehicle charging rate, a change to the electric Fuel Cost and Purchased Power Adjustment rider "which will allow for recovery of all purchased power costs," and the 2027 MLGW budget.
  • The official documents conflict on the public hearing. The committee's resolution says "the City Council shall hold a public hearing on November 24, 2026 at 4:00 P.M. to convene as a Ratemaking Board." The revised full Council agenda (file dated Fri Nov 20, re-checked Sun Nov 22) shows "PUBLIC HEARING (None)" and "MLGW DIVISION (None)," with no rate items. The Council's video calendar lists only the regular 4:00 PM meeting, with no separate hearing. A Nov 18 special MLGW committee agenda listed a resolution "Setting date for the Regular Meeting at which the full Council will consider the Proposed Rate Increase," but no outcome is posted.
