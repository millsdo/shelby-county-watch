The big day is Wednesday, Nov 18. County Commission committees (8:30 AM to about 1:30 PM) take up the County data center standards ordinance (first reading) and the data center moratorium (second reading), both sent back to committee on Oct 26. Also on the list: a resolution moving full Commission meetings from 3:00 PM to 1:00 PM for good, two emergency jail contracts, a $522,500 emergency elevator repair at 160 N. Main, a $2.66 million single-source Oracle consulting contract, and the Judicial Commissioner appointment. Arlington: the County's household hazardous waste agreement with Arlington and the other towns is on third reading ($0.25 per resident), and the Collierville-Arlington Road bridge right-of-way goes to the State. The Arlington Planning Commission meets Mon Nov 16 with no agenda posted. Millington's Planning Commission holds its data center ordinance public hearing Mon Nov 16. MSCS votes Tue Nov 17 on the consent contracts previewed Nov 10. The MLGW Board (Wed Nov 18) takes up up to $325 million in water and gas bonds; no rate change is on the agenda.

Data centers: County standards ordinance, first reading

CountyShelby County Commission, General Government committeeWed Nov 18, about 10:00 AM

“ORDINANCE - FIRST READING – Ordinance amending Ordinance No. 397, known as the Memphis and Shelby County Unified Development Code, to establish regulations for the siting ... of large-scale Date Centers”
Agenda
Details
  • Sponsors: Commissioners Huett-Garcia, Streeter, Rudd, Sugarmon, Brooks, Richardson, Caswell and Bibbs. "(DEFERRED BACK TO COMMITTEE IN OCTOBER 26, 2026 COMMISSION MEETING)"
  • The draft covers sites with a projected demand of "twenty (20) megawatts or greater." It requires closed-loop cooling, bans routine evaporative cooling, and phases in renewable or non-carbon power: "Not less than sixty percent (60%) of annual electricity consumption shall be matched" at start, rising to 100% after ten years, with carbon-based power phased out by year 10.
  • These standards would apply to new data center investment, which helps the County's tax base.
