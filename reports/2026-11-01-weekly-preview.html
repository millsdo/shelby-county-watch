Shelby County Weekly Meeting Preview: Nov 2 - 8, 2026

Week of Mon Nov 2 - Sun Nov 8, 2026 · All times CT · Refreshed Sun Nov 1, 2026, about 6:15 PM CT

Every quote is copied from the posted agenda or packet unless marked as a calendar listing or news report. "Confirmed" means the body has posted an agenda or notice with the date and time; "Expected" means the date comes from a calendar listing or regular schedule only. Red flags are leads for review, not accusations.

What changed since the Fri Oct 30 morning check: The Arlington Board of Mayor and Aldermen agenda for Mon Nov 2 is posted. It elects a Vice Mayor and puts a new Public Comment Policy, a readopted Open Records Policy and the Summer Square development agreement on the consent agenda, with a public hearing on a second town bank depository. The County Commission has no meetings this week; its committees meet next on Wed Nov 18 (the deferred data center moratorium and standards are expected then) and the full Commission on Mon Nov 23. The MLGW Board (Wed Nov 4) posted its agenda: no rate changes, but plans for up to $325 million in new gas and water revenue bonds and a 31-year discounted gas supply contract. The Election Commission called an election-night special meeting (Tue Nov 3, 7 PM) to draw precincts for the post-election audit. New agendas also came in for the Collierville Planning Commission (Thu Nov 5) and the Memphis Alcohol Commission (Wed Nov 4), and Bartlett replaced its Nov 2 Planning Commission agenda with the full packet. The Downtown Design Review Board meeting on Nov 4 is canceled. There is no full Memphis Council meeting this week (committees only on Election Day); the Council's last data center work session is Mon Nov 9 and the next full Council is Tue Nov 10.

MLGW: up to $325 million in gas and water revenue bonds; 31-year discounted gas supply deal

MemphisMLGW Board of CommissionersWed Nov 4

“Resolution approving plans to issue tax-exempt Series 2027 Water System Revenue Bonds in an original aggregated principal amount of not to exceed $225,000,000.00.”
Show full quotes
“Resolution approving plans to issue tax-exempt Series 2027 Water System Revenue Bonds in an original aggregated principal amount of not to exceed $225,000,000.00.”
“Resolution approving plans to issue tax-exempt Series 2027 Gas System Revenue Bonds in an original aggregated principal amount of not to exceed $100,000,000.00.”
Agenda
Details
  • Both are on consent, "REQUIRES CITY COUNCIL APPROVAL", and let MLGW reimburse itself from bond proceeds for capital spending already made. Ratepayers repay revenue bonds. A companion item picks the underwriters, bond counsel and financial advisors.
  • Item 1, a gas supply contract with Tennergy Corporation for up to 31 years and up to 35,000 MMBtu a day, at a discount of at least 35 cents per MMBtu at the start. MLGW says it "benefits our customers with discounts on their gas bill of potentially $3.3 million annually."
  • No rate changes are on this agenda. The three-year gas and water rate increases in MLGW's 2027 budget (approved by the MLGW Board Oct 21) still need City Council approval.
