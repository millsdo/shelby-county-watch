Shelby County Weekly Update by Organization: Oct 19–25, 2026

Week of Mon Oct 19 – Sun Oct 25, 2026, with a look-ahead to Mon Oct 26 · All times CT · Updated Tue Oct 20, 2026, 4:25 PM CT

What changed and how to read this report

What changed since Sunday (Oct 18, 6:10 PM CT): Millington called a special Board of Mayor and Aldermen meeting for Thu Oct 22 at 6:00 PM to approve an on-demand transit deal with Delta Human Resource Agency (city share up to $50,000 a year plus up to $15,000 for fuel). Millington Schools added a special called meeting the same evening for its TISA accountability report. Lakeland posted its Oct 22 agenda: a $1,475,327.50 Canada Road paving contract with only one bidder, and a $153,681.65 inspection contract awarded without a stated competition. Memphis posted a substitute data center moratorium (Ord. 5982, now up to 18 months) and the text of the data center zoning ordinance (Ord. 5987) before Tuesday's Council vote. EDGE, the Port, and EDFC posted Wednesday agendas: a $4.55M tax break for ITT Goulds Pumps, a waiver of affordable-unit requirements for The Grove, and a lapsed Port lease whose new terms say only "See attached". Collierville posted its Oct 26 agenda. The County created its Oct 26 full Commission meeting (no agenda yet). The city calendar moved the Council's data center discussion session from Oct 27 to Mon Oct 26, matching Granicus.

This report is organized by organization. Each section lists changes since Sunday first, then meetings, then watch-list items with the quoted agenda line, then red flags with amounts checked against the official document. "Confirmed" means the body's own posting gives the date and time; "Expected" means the date comes from a calendar, legal notice, or regular schedule only. Every quote is copied from the posted agenda or packet. Red flags are leads for review, not accusations.

“no application subject to this Section shall be processed, scheduled, heard, approved, or issued during the moratorium”
“Eighteen (18) months”
Agenda
Details
  • Agenda item 19, held from Sep 15 and Oct 6. Sponsors: Smiley, Green, Swearengen-Washington, Easter-Thomas.
  • What it would do: freeze zoning, land use, building, and infrastructure permits for data centers for up to 18 months. "Data center" includes "artificial intelligence processing, cryptocurrency mining, commercial server farming".

Memphis data center zoning districts, Ord. 5987 and Ord. 5988 (2nd reading, consent) (Ord. 5987 text new Oct 20)

MemphisMemphis City CouncilTue Oct 20, 4:00 PM

“self-supplied or behind-the-meter facilities ... are prohibited”
Agenda
Details
  • Consent items 9–10. Ord. 5987 (Warren, Canale, Spinosa, Carlisle, White) creates light (LDC) and heavy (HDC) data center districts. A "Large Data Center" or HyperScale facility is 20 MW or more. Behind-the-meter power is banned in light districts and needs a Conditional Use Permit in heavy districts. Energy production includes "gas turbines"; fossil fuel production is "discouraged".
  • Ord. 5988 (Logan, Cooper-Sutton, Swearengen-Washington, Walker, Warren) sets "a Land-Use and Siting Framework for Data Centers and other Resource-Intensive Uses".
  • The posted Ord. 5987 is a scanned image; quotes come from a text reading of the scan.
  • Also item 33: a Cooper-Sutton resolution "requesting comprehensive data center standards to protect Memphis Residents, Ratepayers, and Public Resources" (held from Oct 6).
