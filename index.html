“8.D) Request for Approval to Create and Fund a Temporary Human Resources Manager (Designee) Position”
“9.C) Contract Amendment and Renewal for Sanitation Automated Routing Software”
Agenda
Details
  • Consent: Resolution 2026-33 appropriates $82,639.26 of General Fund unassigned fund balance for the temporary HR position (Nov 2026 - June 2027); staff expect "actual incremental expenditure" of $0.00 because of vacancy savings.

  • Also: Brenntag lime and DivcoData utility billing renewals, Ingram library materials renewal, $302,570.80 in library donations, and cancellation of the Dec 28 meeting.
